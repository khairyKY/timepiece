## Reference movement: Unitas / ETA 6497-2
36.6 mm × 4.5 mm · **21,600 vph = 3 Hz** · 53 h power reserve · 17 jewels · small seconds at 9 (the 6498 puts it at 6).
Sources: [Worn & Wound](https://wornandwound.com/caliber-spec-unitas-64976498/) · [Caliber Corner](https://calibercorner.com/unitas-caliber-6497/) · [Grail Wiki](https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/ETA_6497)

| Wheel | Teeth | Pinion | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrel | 75 (**stand-in**, Marvin 18,000 vph train) | – | 1 rev / 7.5 h |
| Centre | 80 | 10 (**stand-in**) | 1 rev / h (minute hand) |
| Third | 60 | 10 | 1 rev / 7.5 min |
| Fourth | 120 | 8 | 1 rev / min (seconds) |
| Escape | 15 (club teeth) | 10 | 1 rev / 5 s |

Arithmetic: 80/10 × 60/8 = **60**. 120/10 = 12 escape turns/min × 15 teeth × 2 beats = 360 beats/min = **21,600 vph**. Each beat advances the escape wheel half a tooth = **12°**.
Train source: [horology-student.org](http://horology-student.org/movements/modern-eta-and-clones/unitas-eta-6497-6498/) (ETA training-tool version). Barrel stand-in: [vintagewatchstraps](https://www.vintagewatchstraps.com/watchmovement2.php).

Motion works: standard 3 × 4 = 12 : 1 (cannon 12 → minute wheel 36, minute pinion 10 → hour wheel 40). Not 6497-verified.

## Escapement
- Lift angle **44°** for the 6497 ([Caliber Corner](https://calibercorner.com/unitas-caliber-6497/)); 52° is the modern generic.
- Balance ≈ simple harmonic, amplitude ~270–280°; θ(t) = A·cos(2πft), f = vph / 7200.
- Per beat: unlock (tiny recoil) → impulse → drop → lock. Wheel moves ~5% of the beat, rests ~95%. ([Wikipedia: Lever escapement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lever_escapement))

## Moon phase
- 59-tooth disc, two moons, pushed once a day: 1 day error every 2 y 7 m 20 d. 135-tooth "astronomical": 1 day / 122 y. ([FHH](https://www.hautehorlogerie.org/en/watches-and-culture/watchmaking-knowledge/encyclopedia/moonphases-complications))
- Ours is set from the moon's computed position, so it has no mechanical drift.
- Window: semicircle with two humps ("bosoms") that hide the moon on entry/exit.

## Finishing (approximate)
Côtes de Genève band ~2–3 mm · perlage circles ~0.8–1.5 mm, overlapping ⅓ · blued screws 290 °C ≈ `#1B2A6B`, 300 °C ≈ `#3A5BB8` · gold ≈ `#F9E4A4` (measured, linear 0.944/0.776/0.373) · rhodium ≈ `#E0E0E0` · ruby ≈ `#B0103A`.
Aventurine: glass + copper crystals 0.1–0.5 mm, flat, roughly parallel; blue = cobalt glass. ([Wikipedia: Goldstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldstone_(glass)))

## Tech
- `three@0.186.1` (pin — r187 changes PMREM). `WebGLRenderer`, not WebGPU. Imports via `three/addons/*`.
- `NeutralToneMapping` (Khronos PBR Neutral) for product colour. `RoomEnvironment` + PMREM `fromScene(env, 0.04)`.
- `ExtrudeGeometry` bevel defaults are huge at mm scale — always pass tiny bevels.
- Material `anisotropy` direction comes from UVs; lathe U runs circumferentially → circular brushing.
- `THREE.Timer` replaces the deprecated `Clock` (r183). `MathUtils.damp` for frame-rate-independent smoothing.
- **suncalc 2.0.2** (BSD-2, ~3.6 KB gz): **v2 is breaking** — degrees not radians, azimuth from north, altitude includes refraction, non-events return `null`. `getMoonIllumination → {fraction, phase, angle, waxing}`.
- Timezone → coords: no maintained package; build a table from IANA `zone.tab` + legacy aliases (~6 KB gz). Chrome/Safari return legacy IDs (`Asia/Calcutta`).
- Twilight thresholds: sunrise/sunset −0.833° · civil −6° · nautical −12° · astronomical −18°.
