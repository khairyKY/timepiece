# Research: Piaget Polo Skeleton G0A46009 (2026-09-28)

Gathered by a research agent + the reference photo (Swisswatches Magazine). Tags: **OFF** = Piaget official (Wayback copy of the product page, 11 Dec 2023), **PRESS** = press, **OBS** = observed on official images, **EST** = estimated from the photo. Anything not tagged OFF/PRESS is modelled, and the page says so.

## Identity
- **G0A46009**: 18k rose gold, blue skeleton. Announced April 2021, delivered from September 2021. CHF 43,100 at launch. 67.2 g of gold (OFF). Strap only, no bracelet.
- Siblings: G0A45001 (steel, slate grey), G0A45004 (steel, blue), G0A46010 (white gold, 56 diamonds), G0A47008 (steel, green).

## Case
- **42 mm × 6.5 mm** (OFF). Case shape per Piaget: "cushion", a "shape-in-shape" design.
- Bezel top satin-brushed **east to west** (straight, horizontal). Polished bevel on the outer edge; thin polished chamfer on the inner edge of the opening (PRESS/OBS).
- Opening ≈ **33.4 mm** across the flats (EST from the photo at 12.7 px/mm). Modelled as a superellipse, a = 16.7, n = 3.3.
- Case band **horizontally brushed** with polished chamfers. Short integrated lugs taper with polished bevels. Quick-release spring bars; the pushers are visible from the back (OBS).
- Strap ≈ 21–22 mm (EST). Lug-to-lug 47 mm (dealer figure, unverified).
- Crown at 3, rose gold. Notched/fluted rim; flat end face with a serif **P** in a circular medallion (OBS).
- Caseback: sapphire in a wide **brushed** gold ring with **6 screws at 12/2/4/6/8/10**. Engraved: PIAGET (~11), Au750 (~9), hallmarks, SWISS MADE split by the 6 o'clock screw, and a serial number (OBS). Serials are not reproduced.
- Flat sapphire crystal (OBS). 3 ATM / 30 m (OFF).

## Calibre 1200S1
- **31.9 mm, 2.4 mm, 21,600 vph / 3 Hz, ~44 h, 25 jewels** (OFF); **189 components** (PRESS). Hours and minutes only.
- **Micro-rotor** off-centre and flush with the plate: it sits inside the movement's own height and shows from both sides. Slate grey, with the coat of arms, MANUFACTURE and PIAGET engraved on its back face. Material disputed (tungsten vs platinum) (PRESS/OFF).
- Blue PVD on the mainplate and bridges. Wheels silver-grey (OBS).
- Finishing (OFF): circular and sunburst satin wheels; sunburst satin plate and bridges; bevelled plate and bridges; polished blind screws with bevelled slots; grey screws.
- The hour-wheel bridge is **0.11 mm** at its narrowest (PRESS).
- **Front layout** (PRESS + OBS):
  - 12–1: openworked **barrel**, mainspring visible, under a three-armed curved blue bridge.
  - 3: keyless works.
  - Centre: hour and minute wheels.
  - ~5: escape wheel and pallet lever.
  - 6: **balance** under a bridge.
  - 8–10: **micro-rotor**, seen through an arched aperture.
- **Back layout**: micro-rotor. A transmission wheel by the crown drives two intermediates, which turn the ratchet wheel over the barrel. The automatic train sits above the rotor. Escape wheel, pallet and balance at the bottom.
- Tooth counts are **unpublished**. The train is modelled on the standard 3 Hz arithmetic (see `research-2026-09-28.md`) and labelled as modelled on the page.

## Dial side
- Blue **flange** carrying 60 white minute ticks, PIAGET along the curve at ~10:30–11, and SWISS MADE split by the 6 o'clock index (OBS/PRESS).
- **12 identical applied rose-gold indices**: tapered, wide outside and pointed inside, a central ridge with two facets. Fixed to the flange at the outer end, cantilevered over the movement (PRESS/OBS).
- **Hands**: gold, faceted, **openworked alpha/sword**, no lume. **No seconds hand** (OFF/PRESS).

## Strap
- Blue alligator, tool-free quick release; 18k rose-gold folding clasp (OFF/PRESS).
